I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at Iowa State University, where I explore the intersection of **artificial intelligence, computer vision, and scientific image analysis**.

My research focuses on developing intelligent methods that combine machine learning, foundation models, vision-language models, and generative AI to help interpret complex scientific images and support data-driven discovery.

## Research Interests

- **Computer Vision:** Image understanding, visual recognition, and scientific image analysis.
- **Foundation Models:** Adapting and evaluating pretrained models for specialized scientific tasks.
- **Vision-Language Models:** Connecting visual information with language-based reasoning and decision support.
- **Generative AI:** Exploring generative models for scientific imaging and data-driven research.

## Research Highlights

My work explores how AI can help researchers extract meaningful information from complex microscopy images.

- **AI-Based Defect Classification:** Developing conversational LLM-based decision support for interpreting defects in atomic force microscopy (AFM) images.
- **Scientific Image Understanding:** Investigating vision-language models and AI-driven methods for microscopy image analysis.
- **Generative Scientific Imaging:** Exploring generative models to study and synthesize scientific image structures.

## Beyond Research

Outside research, I enjoy practicing Taekwondo, exploring cycling, and photography. Martial arts continually remind me of the value of discipline, patience, and consistent improvement—qualities that are just as important when solving challenging research problems.

*Curious by nature. Research-driven by passion. Always learning.*
